# Privacy Policy – Clipper Scheduler

Clipper Scheduler is a private tool used only by the owner of the
DailyClipzGaming channels to upload their own video clips to
YouTube, TikTok and Instagram via the official APIs.

- The only data accessed is the owner's own account data needed to upload videos.
- No data from other users is collected, stored, sold or shared.
- Access tokens are stored securely on the owner's private server.
- Access can be revoked at any time via the platform's account settings
  (e.g. myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Contact: via the DailyClipzGaming YouTube channel.
